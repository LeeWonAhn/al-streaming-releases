What is AL Streaming?

AL Streaming turns the videos you already have into continuous livestreams. Pick a video or a playlist, add the stream keys of your channels, press Start, and the app keeps your live running — looping your content and reconnecting on its own if the connection drops.

It runs on your own computer. Your videos never leave your machine except as the live stream you send to each platform.

Features
	
Loop videos and playlists	Stream one video or a whole playlist on repeat, for as long as you need.
YouTube, Facebook and TikTok	Send the same content to several platforms at the same time using their official stream keys (RTMP).
Vertical output	Vertical framing for TikTok LIVE, YouTube Shorts live and Facebook Reels live.
Auto-reconnect	If the network or the platform drops the connection, the app retries automatically and keeps your stream going.
Library and playlists	Keep your videos organized and reuse them across streams.
Live history	See when each stream ran and how long it stayed live.
Secure by design	Stream keys are encrypted with your operating system's secure storage (Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain) and are never written to logs.
Automatic updates	The app updates itself from this page. No reinstalling.
English and Vietnamese	Choose your language at first launch and switch anytime in Settings.

Setup guide
Windows
Run the .exe installer you downloaded.
If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
Follow the installer, then open AL Streaming from the Start menu.
macOS
Open the .dmg file and drag AL Streaming into Applications.
Open AL Streaming from Applications.
If macOS says the app can't be opened, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
First launch
Choose your language (English or Tiếng Việt).
Activate the app with your activation key (format ALS-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). No key yet? Click Buy activation key — see below.
Add your videos in Library & Playlist.
Add destinations in Destinations: paste the stream URL and stream key from YouTube Studio, Facebook Live Producer or TikTok LIVE.
Start streaming from Streams: choose a video or playlist, choose the destinations, press Start.

Tip: each live stream uses your upload bandwidth. Use a wired connection when you can, and keep the computer awake while streaming.
